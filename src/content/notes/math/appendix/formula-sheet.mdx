### Logarithms and exponentials

$$
e^{a}e^{b} = e^{a+b}, \qquad \ln(ab) = \ln a + \ln b, \qquad \ln(a^k) = k\ln a, \qquad \log_b x = \frac{\ln x}{\ln b}.
$$

### Trigonometry

$$
\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1, \qquad \sin(a \pm b) = \sin a\cos b \pm \cos a\sin b, \qquad \cos 2x = \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x.
$$

Euler's formula links the two worlds: $e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta$.

### Matrices

$$
(AB)^\top = B^\top A^\top, \qquad (AB)^{-1} = B^{-1}A^{-1}, \qquad \det(AB) = \det A \,\det B.
$$

For a $2 \times 2$ matrix with $\det = ad - bc \ne 0$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad - bc}\begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}.
$$

The trace (sum of diagonal entries) equals the sum of the eigenvalues, and the determinant equals their product. See [Eigenvalues](/workbench/math/linear-algebra/eigenvalues-and-eigenvectors/).

Gradients of common vector expressions, from [Matrix calculus](/workbench/math/calculus/matrix-calculus/):

$$
\nabla_{\mathbf{x}}(\mathbf{a}^\top\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{a}, \qquad \nabla_{\mathbf{x}}(\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x}) = (A + A^\top)\mathbf{x}, \qquad \nabla_{\mathbf{x}}\lVert A\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{b}\rVert^2 = 2A^\top(A\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{b}).

$$

### Probability

$$
P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B), \qquad E[aX + b] = aE[X] + b, \qquad \operatorname{Var}(aX + b) = a^2\operatorname{Var}(X).
$$

$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}, \qquad P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A)\,P(A)}{P(B)}, \qquad \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!}.
$$

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X + Y) = \operatorname{Var}(X) + \operatorname{Var}(Y) + 2\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y), \qquad \operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n) = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}.
$$

<Callout type="pitfall">
The quotient rule has the numerator $f'g - fg'$, not $f'g' - fg$, and the chain rule multiplies by the inner derivative even when it is not written. Writing $\ln\lvert x\rvert$ rather than $\ln x$ in the integral is what makes $\frac{1}{x}$ integrate correctly for negative $x$.
</Callout>
